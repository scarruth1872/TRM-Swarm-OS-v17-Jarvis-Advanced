# Recommendations — implemented, with the evidence

**1. `RETIRED` state and a mandatory reason.** `POST /kanban/cards/{id}/move` now accepts `RETIRED`, and a
terminal move (`DONE` or `RETIRED`) without a `reason` is refused with 400. The card stores `reason`,
`reason_at` and `closed_at`; `retired_count` appears in `/kanban/stats`; retired cards are excluded from
`stale_count` and from the closer's open set; the board carries a `RETIRED` column. Verified all four gate
cases — no reason → 400, DONE without reason → 400, malformed body → 400, RETIRED with a UTF-8 reason → 200.
Seven cards retired with their measured reasons (02, 03, 04, 08, 09, 11, 13); board went from a false
24/24 done to **6 todo / 11 done / 7 retired**.

*A second defect surfaced while testing this*: an unparseable move body used to fall back to
`target_status: "TODO"` and answer 200 — a malformed write silently moved the card. It now refuses.

**2. Telegram transport health.** `tools/cron/telegram_transport.py`, job `09560fd08024`, hourly at :15,
delivered to the DM. Silent when healthy; one line plus the specific problem when not (any failed send,
failures with zero recoveries, flapping above baseline, or 4+ injected notices in the hour). Baseline it
measures against: 222 failures / 222 recoveries in one day.

**3. Background-notice volume.** The gateway has no batching option, so the reduction is a source rule
(`docs/AGENT-COORDINATION.md`) and the volume is now counted every hour by the job above. Measured today:
8 notices → 8 turns.

**4. Self-citation excluded from the index.** `tools/build_council_index.py` now applies `SELF_CITATION_GLOBS`
**by default** (evaluation reports that quote the questions, council findings, and council output), with
`--allow-self-citation` to reproduce the old failure deliberately. Rebuilt as `v3`: **2,385 chunks, 0 from any
excluded pattern**, against 46 such chunks in the shipped v1. Re-scoring the gate remains the gate owner's
call — it needs a fresh pre-registration, not a retrospective one.

**5. A digest that reaches Telegram.** `tools/cron/telegram_digest.py`, job `6995397e096f`, daily at 07:00,
`deliver: telegram:8047398662`. Fired once to prove delivery: `last_status ok`,
`last_delivery_error: null`.
