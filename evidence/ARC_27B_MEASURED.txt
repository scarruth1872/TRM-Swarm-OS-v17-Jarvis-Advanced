=== HONEST INTERPRETATION ===
  1/3 exact is a REAL improvement over the TRM's 0/3 on the same tasks,
  but it is ONE task and n=3. This is a signal, not a benchmark result.

  2. The 27B is 10-100x SLOWER: 35-97s vs the TRM's 0.74-3.1s.
  3. The TRM's cell accuracy (91.75% on 00d62c1b) is HIGHER than the 27B's
     there (0% - wrong shape). The TRM fails differently: near-miss grids,
     the 27B fails differently: wrong dimensions or nothing at all.

  4. Therefore they are NOT interchangeable. The 27B is better at exact
     solution, the TRM is better at partial coverage and 30x faster.
     A router could use the TRM for the shape and the 27B for the content -
     but that is a design proposal, not a measured result.

=== ROOT CAUSE OF THE TRUNCATION - FOUND AFTER THE RUN ===
  The llama-server log for 00d62c1b shows:
    total time = 96949.96 ms / 4096 tokens
    stop processing: n_tokens = 4095, truncated = 1

  It hit --ctx-size 4096, NOT n_predict. trm/llama server ctx includes the
  prompt (~1100-1300 tokens for these grids), so raising n_predict from
  700 to 2400 changed nothing - the ceiling was the context window all along.

  So my 'output length is NOT the limiting factor' statement above was
  WRONG. Length IS the factor; I adjusted the wrong parameter and then
  concluded the hypothesis was dead. Correct fix: --ctx-size 8192+.


  THE 1/3 RESULT IS THEREFORE A LOWER BOUND, not the model's ceiling.
  Retest with --ctx-size 8192 before drawing any conclusion.
