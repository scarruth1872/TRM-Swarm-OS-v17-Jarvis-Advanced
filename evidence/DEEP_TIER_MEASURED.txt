DEFECT FOUND WHILE WIRING: the deep answer was truncated before it existed
  Bonsai is a reasoning model: it emits a </think> block BEFORE the answer.
  At n_predict=192 the reply ended mid-thought and the caller never saw an
  answer at all:
      '</think>\nHere's a thinking process:\n\n1. **Analyze User Input:** ...'
  Deep calls now use max(n_predict, 640) so the reasoning plus the answer fit.

COST / TRADE-OFF (measured, not estimated)
  Deep questions now take ~45s instead of ~18.5s of truncated output or ~1.5s of
  1.5B output. That is the honest price of a 27B reasoning model on a 6700 XT at
  Q1_0 with a 640-token budget. It is a DEEP tier: slow, deliberate, and only on
  explicit deep-word or long-question routing. If latency matters more than depth,
  the knob is the budget and the tier, not the wiring.

STILL OPEN
  Bonsai-27B uses ~4 GB of the 12 GB VRAM alongside the 1.5B on 8587. Fits, but
  there is no headroom check: if another GPU consumer starts, the deep tier is
  the first thing to fail, and voice.py will silently fall back to 8587.
  Ternary-Bonsai 1.7B/4B/8B remain unloadable (tensor offset mismatch), so 27B is
  the only ternary model available for this tier.
