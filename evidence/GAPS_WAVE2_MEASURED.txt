  FAILURE FOUND AND FIXED BY THE PARENT: after the agent exited, port 3002 was
  DOWN (connection refused) and 3001 returned 502 -- the proxy was failing
  because its upstream bridge was gone. The agent had started the bridge as a
  child of its own session, which dies when the agent exits. Re-started detached
  from the parent session: 3002 up in 1s, 3001 returned to 200.
  This is the second time a subagent's restarted service died with the agent
  (the first was 8022 in the G9 wave). Restart procedures for subagents must use
  DETACHED_PROCESS + CREATE_NEW_PROCESS_GROUP, not nohup from the agent session.
