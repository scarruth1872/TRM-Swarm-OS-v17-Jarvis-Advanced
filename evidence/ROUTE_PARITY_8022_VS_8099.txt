Measured 2026-11-06 against the live stack (8022 host_council_server, 8099 python app_v2).

METHOD
  Every @router.<verb>("/path") declaration in swarm_v2/routes/*.py was extracted
  mechanically (268 declarations across 22 route modules) and probed on BOTH ports with the
  declared verb. A path counts as answered when the port returns status < 400.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE PLAN
  The porting job is smaller than the raw 277-route count suggested. 84 of the 268 paths
  already answer on the Rust council today - kanban (19), mesh (9), batch_endpoints (9),
  swarm (7), artifacts and learning and system (6 each). The genuine Python-only mass is
  concentrated: swarm 38 of 65, system 18 of 21, kanban 11 of 21 still answered only by
  Python, plus four modules that answer nowhere at all (changelog 10/14, insights 6/10,
  untapped 5/16, coordination 1/3).

  Two caveats, stated rather than hidden:
    - "answers" means status < 400, not "returns the documented shape". Parity by shape is
      still unmeasured and remains P1's real work.
    - 117 of the 268 declarations answer on neither port; some need path parameters or a
      body this probe did not supply, so they are not necessarily dead. A parameterised
      probe is required before anything is retired.
