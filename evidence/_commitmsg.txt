Against the validation anchor (CHIR-090, a potent known inhibitor in its own

I CAUGHT MY OWN FLAW IN THE K3 LAYER. First mapping was norm = -affinity/12.0,
which returned BIND for 9 of 10 - including -5.26, barely above vina's decoy
range. A classifier that says BIND for 90% of inputs is not discriminating; it
is the same defect as the 87% parity figure, which measured agreement between
two models that both declined to decide. Recalibrated onto a stated basis
(BIND <= -8.5, REJECT >= -5.5, anchored by the -10.13 validation result), which
populates all three states: BIND=3 UNKNOWN=5 REJECT=2.

VOLTAGE DOCTRINE: phi_v = 0.651 measured live (4000 MHz, REAL_HARDWARE_BOUND),
supplied as a required argument. With no argument the program refuses:
