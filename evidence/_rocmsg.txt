feat(phoenix): decoy/ROC FALSIFIES the -8.5 threshold; re-judge the verdicts

THE EXPERIMENT: 42 ligands docked against 5U39 (P. aeruginosa LpxC) on the
validated box, exhaustiveness 8, seed 42 -- 2 CRYSTAL-CONFIRMED actives
(CHIR-090 from 5U39, NVS-LPXC-01 from 5U3B) versus 40 property-matched decoys
(MW +/-20%, logP +/-1, HBD/HBA +/-1, rotB +/-3, ECFP4 Tanimoto < 0.35).
Actives were built FROM SMILES so they go through the identical pipeline as the
decoys; a crystal pose would have rigged the comparison in their favour.

RESULT -- the stated threshold did not survive measurement:

  rank 1  ACTIVE  CHIR-090      -10.170
  rank 2  decoy   decoy_037      -8.541   <- a decoy
  ...
  rank 8  ACTIVE  NVS-LPXC-01    -8.087   <- a crystal-confirmed active

  decoys beating the WEAKER active : 6 of 40
  decoys beating the STRONGER one  : 0 of 40
  best decoy -8.541 sits BETWEEN the two actives.

  AUC 0.925 (n=2 actives: LOW CONFIDENCE)
  exact Mann-Whitney U = 74/80, p = 0.0186 over all 861 label splits
  active ranks: 1 and 8 of 42

So -8.5, which I had used as the PHOENIX BIND cut, ADMITS A DECOY AS BIND. It is
falsified. The threshold moves to -9.0, where zero decoys pass -- accepting the
cost of dropping one true active, because for a triage step a missed candidate is
cheaper than a fabricated one.

APPLIED BACK: two PHOENIX BIND verdicts are downgraded.
  IFIH1 -8.610  BIND -> UNKNOWN   (inside the measured decoy range)
  TYK2  -8.579  BIND -> UNKNOWN   (inside the measured decoy range)
  BLK   -9.057  BIND -> BIND      (survives, by 0.057)
Revised ternary summary: BIND=1 UNKNOWN=7 REJECT=2 (was BIND=3 UNKNOWN=5 REJECT=2)

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
  The protocol DOES rank a strong binder cleanly: CHIR-090 sits at rank 1 with
  0/40 decoys above it, and its SMILES-built conformer redocked to -10.170 versus
  -10.13 from the crystal pose -- a 0.04 agreement that independently
  cross-validates the pipeline.
  It does NOT separate a moderate binder: 6 decoys outrank NVS-LPXC-01. With two
  actives the AUC is not a stable estimate and is reported with that flag.
  No single vina threshold separates this active set from these decoys, so every
  BIND verdict resting on a bare score is weak evidence, and the journal says so.

ALSO FIXED: my ROC analyzer had the sign convention INVERTED -- it stayed silent
while the data plainly showed overlap. Affinities are negative, so overlap means
best_decoy < worst_act, and it was testing the opposite. It now reports the count
of decoys above each active and states plainly when a threshold admits decoys.
A check that cannot report the failure it exists to catch is not a check.
