## Task 463 - 3D reconstruction for protein structures: DONE, verified

Source: the platform's own 9 PDB structures from WSL `/home/shawn/docking` (copied to
`scratch/task_463_structures/`). Method: extract the CA trace, build the inter-residue
distance matrix, reconstruct 3D coordinates **from distances alone** by classical metric
MDS, then score against the deposited coordinates after optimal superposition.

| regime | result |
|---|---|
| exact distances known | **mean RMSD 0.000000 A over 9/9** - reconstruction is exact |
| distances > 8 A withheld (bridged by the triangle inequality) | **17.724 A over 8/9**; 2RD0 reported `n/a` |
| binary contact map only (< 8 A) | **13.585 A over 8/9** |

2RD0 is **not reconstructable** from the 8 A regime - its contact graph is disconnected
(chain breaks up to 54.7 A) - and is reported as such with its unreachable-pair count
rather than emitting a number. Chirality: **2/9 reconstructions came out mirrored**. A
distance matrix determines geometry but not handedness; that is a property of distance
geometry, not a defect, and is reported per structure.

Two ruler faults were found and fixed before any of this was published:
1. Forcing det=+1 in the superposition made mirrored reconstructions look like 14-18 A
   errors. Both handedness options must be scored.
2. The `UNKNOWN` sentinel leaked through the truncated regimes and produced a 414,224 A
   "result" for 2RD0. Unreachable pairs must be detected and reported as not
   reconstructable.

Artifacts: `scratch/run_task_463_protein_3d_reconstruction.py`,
`task_463_protein_3d_reconstruction.{json,csv}`.

## Task 466 - feature extraction for docking: DONE

The ChEMBL 37 SDF carries **no SMILES tag at all** (only `> <chembl_id>`); the molecular
graph is in the V2000 connection tables, and that is what the extractor parses. Features,
all derived from the graph: heavy atoms, Hill formula, MW, bonds, rings (cyclomatic),
aromatic bonds, components, HBD, HBA, rotatable bonds (single, non-ring, both ends degree
>= 2), and a Morgan-style circular fingerprint (radius 2, stable FNV-1a 64).

Validated by hand before the full run: benzene C6H6 / 78.11 / 1 ring / 0 rotatable,
cyclohexane C6H12 / 84.16, butane C4H10 with 1 rotatable, fingerprints deterministic.
That check caught a real bug - the rotatable-bond test was inverted (a ring bond is *not*
a bridge, so "not a bridge" means "in a ring", the opposite of rotatable).

Corpus: **2,897,819 molecules / 2,897,818 feature rows**, matching the index's molecule
count exactly. Whole-corpus aggregates: mean MW 444.4, mean HBD 2.05, mean HBA 7.24, mean
rotatable 7.80, max rings 801.

Artifacts: `scratch/extract_molecular_features.py`,
`scratch/task_466_molecular_features_full.csv` (196,727,555 B, sha256 `78c3a68ddb064d2b...`),
`scratch/task_466_molecular_features_sample.csv` (20k rows).

**Defect found in the existing index:** `structured_index.db` `molecule.smiles` is NULL for
**all** 2,897,819 rows and `props` holds the literal text `chembl_id` - the earlier index
build looked for a SMILES tag this source does not contain.

Full-corpus throughput: 2,897,819 records in **834.1 s = 3,474 molecules/s** (13m54s wall),
1 record skipped (malformed connection table) - reported, not hidden.

  **already supports `--mmproj`** - no new build was needed.

  into `C:/Users/shawn/models/gemma-3-4b-it/`.

  (`scratch/task_462_images/sanity_word_number.png`), so the path genuinely decodes pixels
  rather than describing an empty image slot.
- Colony counting against synthetic plates with known truth: 12 -> "21", 27 -> "37".
  A general 4B VLM is not a reliable counter - recorded as measured, not as achieved.
- The third plate (43 colonies) was **never measured**: the server dropped the connection mid-query
  (`ConnectionResetError`, WinError 10054) and the crash preceded the export, so
  `scratch/task_462_colony_counting.json` was reconstructed from that run's stdout with the third row
  preserved as an explicit failure. Re-run the script to complete it.
- **Coexistence fails on this host.** The 8B tier-2 holds 3,075 MiB of 8,192 MiB VRAM; the
  VLM then cannot get a contiguous 2,388 MiB (`cudaMalloc failed: out of memory`) even
  though nvidia-smi reports 4,944 MiB free, and with the projector kept on CPU the 851 MB
  CPU allocation failed too (RAM: 4,853 MiB free of 15,720). Starting the VLM first instead
  evicted the 8B - tier-2 was restored and verified healthy.
- Options: (a) **swap the tiers on demand** - proven to work, the VLM ran alone and served
  real answers; (b) use a much smaller VLM (SmolVLM-500M / moondream, 0.6-1.9 GB) that fits
  alongside tier-2; (c) free host RAM, then retry full GPU offload.
