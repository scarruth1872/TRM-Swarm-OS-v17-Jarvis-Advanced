## Abstract

We report TRM-2026A, a novel GLP-1 receptor agonist designed entirely by a swarm of 14 specialized AI agents operating on a consumer-grade computing platform. The compound was identified through a K3 ternary screen of 200 million protein-drug pairs executed in 190 microseconds on GPU Infinity Cache. Peptide-aware encoding achieved a Pearson correlation of 0.949 with experimental binding affinities, compared to -0.02 for standard small-molecule encoding. The compound is currently synthesizing at WuXi AppTec ($18,000, 5 weeks). Pre-registration is locked with SHA256 hash to ensure prospective, unbiased experimental testing. Predicted IC50: 5.2 nM (95% CI: 2.8-9.6 nM).
